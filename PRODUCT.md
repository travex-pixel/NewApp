# Real Life XP

## Purpose

A social intelligence and decision-making game. Players meet believable real-life situations, choose how they'd respond, see the likely consequences, and pick up one practical, transferable idea about how people work. It is a game first; the learning is embedded in play.

The promise is practice, not measurement. The game never claims to score a person's social intelligence; a score measures performance inside the game's own rules.

## Audience

People arriving from a social link (TikTok, Instagram, a group chat), on a phone, with no context and no obligation. Broad, adult, English-speaking. They may have thirty seconds. Secondary: people sent a challenge link by a friend who want to compare answers.

## Operating context

- Mobile first, in-app browsers included. Portrait, one hand, interrupted attention.
- No account, no email, no onboarding before the first payoff.
- Progress is device-local (localStorage). No cross-device sync; the product says so where it matters.
- Shared challenges carry their state in the URL, so no database is needed for the first release.
- No LLM at runtime. Authored content and deterministic logic only.

## Constraints

- Next.js App Router on Vercel, solo-founder budget, no paid infrastructure for v1.
- Content is the product: every scenario is authored, with trade-offs acknowledged and no invented science.
- No fake social proof, no fake scores, no streak punishment, no energy systems.

## Voice

A perceptive friend, not a teacher and not a chatbot. Specific, dry, warm. Short sentences. Names the trade-off. Praise only when the choice earned it. British spelling.

## Platform

web
